Creation process

In my creation process I use the following steps

1

Knowing the Client and the Company

In the first stage, I seek to gather as much information about the characteristics and personality of the client and the company. A detailed questionnaire with strategic questions about the project.

2

Search

With the information collected, I begin to research the company, the market it operates in, its competitors and target audience. Essential so the creation process is well-founded and not generic.

3

Concept

According to the results obtained in the research, I start a brainstorming process, mapping ideas and drafting concepts, always looking for a greater meaning beyond the visual.

4

Execution

After all the tests, and with an established idea, I start defining typography and colors, in addition to creating shapes, graphics, patterns and support elements.

5

Flexibility

With everything in hand, I begin the application stage in products such as stationery, uniforms, billboards and gifts, ensuring the brand is identified in any situation.

6

Delivery

With the project finalized, I create and send a presentation and the manual for using the brand. Once approved, all files are saved, organized and sent to the customer.
